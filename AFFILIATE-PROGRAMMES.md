# Affiliate programmes

Every vendor covered on Small Crew, its programme status, reported payout and where to apply.

Checked August 2026.

## How to read this file

Payout figures for affiliate programmes are almost never published by the vendor itself. They appear in affiliate network directories and partner marketing pages, and they change without notice. Every figure below is marked with how it was established.

- **Vendor stated** means the figure was read from the vendor's own affiliate page.
- **Third party reported** means the figure comes from affiliate directories and secondary sources, not from the vendor. Treat these as indicative only and confirm on application.
- **None found** means no programme was located in August 2026. It does not prove one does not exist.

No payout figure in this file should be treated as contractual. Confirm terms in the partner dashboard after acceptance.

## Programmes with a confirmed structure

### GorillaDesk

- **Programme:** Affiliate programme, run on FirstPromoter
- **Payout:** 20% recurring commission for 12 months on every new paying customer. Commission increases if the referred customer upgrades plan or adds users. Vendor stated.
- **Attribution:** 14 day free trial, 90 day conversion window
- **Payment:** Monthly, in the first 10 business days
- **Apply:** https://gorilladesk.firstpromoter.com/
- **Programme page:** https://gorilladesk.com/gorilladesk-affiliate-program/
- **Live site required:** No. The vendor states that GorillaDesk membership is not required to participate.
- **Note:** A separate in app referral programme pays existing customers up to $299 per referral. That is a customer referral scheme rather than a publisher programme.

### Jobber

- **Programme:** Jobber Partner Network, run on PartnerStack
- **Payout:** Reported at 60% of the first payment on monthly subscriptions, or fixed amounts on annual plans of $50 for Core, $150 for Connect and $300 for Grow. Third party reported.
- **Attribution:** 30 day cookie
- **Payment:** Net 30 via PayPal or Stripe, $50 minimum balance
- **Apply:** https://dash.partnerstack.com/application?company=jobber&group=jobberpartnernetwork
- **Live site required:** Yes in practice. Applications are reviewed, and the programme states it is looking for partners with an audience of small home service business owners.

### Housecall Pro

- **Programme:** Paid affiliates programme, run on PartnerStack. A separate partner programme also exists.
- **Payout:** Reported at $200 per successful referral on the standard affiliate programme, with higher arrangements reported up to $1,000 upfront through the referral partner route. Third party reported. The brief for this site cited a range of $40 to $400 depending on the plan the referral enrols on. None of these figures could be confirmed on a vendor page in August 2026, and they should be treated as unverified until the partner dashboard shows current terms.
- **Attribution:** Commission reported as earned once the referred account has been on the platform for more than 30 days
- **Apply:** https://www.housecallpro.com/paid-affiliates/
- **Partner programme:** https://www.housecallpro.com/partner/
- **Live site required:** Yes in practice. Applications are reviewed.

### FreshBooks

- **Programme:** Affiliate programme, run on ShareASale
- **Payout:** Reported at up to $10 per free trial and up to $200 per paying customer. Third party reported.
- **Attribution:** 120 day cookie
- **Payment:** 20th of each month, after a 35 day locking period, $50 minimum
- **Apply:** https://www.freshbooks.com/affiliate-program
- **Live site required:** Yes. ShareASale merchants review the applying site before approval.

### QuickBooks

- **Programme:** Affiliate programme
- **Payout:** Reported at $88 per sale and $8 per lead. Third party reported.
- **Attribution:** 45 day cookie
- **Apply:** Through the Intuit affiliate programme
- **Live site required:** Yes, explicitly. Approval requires an active website, blog, YouTube channel or social platform publishing business, accounting or finance content.

## Customer referral schemes, not publisher programmes

These pay existing customers in credit rather than paying publishers in cash. They are recorded here so the distinction is not lost, and they are not a revenue route for this site.

### LawnPro

- **Page:** https://www.lawnprosoftware.com/features/referral-program
- **Publisher value:** None. Credit only.

### Yardbook

- **Programme:** Referral scheme reported as granting a free three month premium subscription per referred friend, up to twelve months across four referrals
- **Publisher value:** None. Credit only.

## Programmes that need a live site before applying

Apply to these only once the site is deployed, indexed and carrying traffic. Applying early risks a rejection that is awkward to reverse.

1. **QuickBooks.** Requires an active website or channel publishing business, accounting or finance content. The strictest requirement of any programme here.
2. **Jobber.** Reviewed applications, and the programme explicitly seeks partners with an audience of home service business owners.
3. **Housecall Pro.** Reviewed applications through PartnerStack.
4. **FreshBooks.** ShareASale merchants review the applying site.

**GorillaDesk is the exception.** Its programme states that no GorillaDesk membership is required, and FirstPromoter programmes generally have a lower approval barrier. It is the sensible first application, and it is also the vendor most frequently recommended across the pest control pages on this site.

## Revenue concentration risk

Worth recording plainly. Of the sixteen vendors covered across the site, five have a locatable publisher programme. Two of those five, Jobber and Housecall Pro, are products this site frequently recommends against at larger crew sizes on cost grounds.

That is a real tension and the resolution is editorial rather than commercial. The disclosure component on every page states that commission does not change the order of the table, and the tables are ordered by fit for a crew under twenty people. Several tools recommended ahead of the commission paying ones, including ZenMaid, Launch27 and Connecteam, pay nothing at all.

If that stops being true, the site has stopped being worth reading.
