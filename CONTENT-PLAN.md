## Publishing order

Ranked by commercial value first, with difficulty used to break ties. Commercial value is monthly volume multiplied by cost per click, which approximates what the traffic is worth rather than how much of it there is.

Lawn care scheduling has 350 searches at a $1.00 cost per click, which is high traffic and low commercial value, so it earns its place as a link source rather than as a revenue page.

## Maintenance

Vendor pricing changes several times a year. Any page whose `pricesChecked` date is more than four months old is stale.

All nineteen pages were checked in **August 2026**. First re verification is due by **December 2026**.

Re verify before doing anything else to a page, and update the date even when nothing has changed.

### Pages carrying unverified figures

These need attention at the next re verification pass regardless of the four month rule.

Cells read `Could not confirm` where no pricing page could be reached, and `Quote only` where a fetched vendor page routes pricing through a sales conversation. The site never asserts that a vendor does not publish pricing unless a fetched page says so.

- **QuickBooks Online**, on `accounting-software-for-cleaning-business`, `lawn-care-billing-software` and `pest-control-accounting-software`. Six separate attempts at Intuit pages all timed out in August 2026. Intuit does publish pricing, so this is a retrieval failure rather than a vendor omission, and it should clear on a manual check.
- **Yardbook**, on `best-lawn-care-software`, `lawn-care-scheduling-software`, `lawn-care-routing-software` and `lawn-care-snow-removal-software`. Every Yardbook URL tried returned HTTP 403, including the homepage and the support site. This is the one vendor whose `url` field is not a page we fetched, because no Yardbook page was reachable at all.
- **Briostack**, on six pest control pages. The reachable page, `briostack.com/additionalpricing`, lists add on and migration charges but no base subscription price.
- **Housecall Pro** seat counts, on seven cleaning and pest control pages. The vendor pricing page publishes three prices and no seat limits, which is a genuine vendor omission rather than a retrieval failure.

### Resolved in the August 2026 audit

- **Swept** was previously shown as unpriced. Its live site is `sweptworks.com`, not `swept.io`, which is now a parked domain that was for sale. Swept publishes Launch at $30, Optimize at $150 and Scale at $225 a month, banded by number of locations.
- **Aspire** now cites `youraspire.com/aspire-plans`, where the vendor states pricing varies by company size and complexity and includes unlimited user licences.
- **PestPac** now cites `workwave.com/industries/pest-control-software`, which shows no price and routes buyers to a demo.
- **ServSuite by FieldRoutes** was confirmed as quote and demo only from its FieldRoutes page.
